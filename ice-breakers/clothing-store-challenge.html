📖 How to Play

  1. Shopping Bag Memory — Flip cards to match each picture with its word.
  2. Drag the Label — Drag the correct word onto the picture of clothing.
  3. Dress the Customer — Listen to what the customer needs, then click the correct color and the correct clothing item.
  4. Opposite Challenge — Choose the word that means the opposite.
  5. Size Challenge — Read the size tag and choose the correct size.
  6. Shopping Speed Round — Answer as many quick questions as you can in 60 seconds!

💡 You have 3 hints for the whole game. 🔊 Use the mute button to turn sounds on or off.
